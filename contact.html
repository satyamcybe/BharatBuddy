FAQ

Common Questions

Is it free to register as a guide? +

Yes! Registering as a local guide on LocalGuide is completely free. You can list your profile, specialities, and languages spoken at no cost.

How do I book a local guide? +

Browse our guides page, click on a guide you like, view their profile, and reach out through our contact page. We'll help connect you with the right person.

Can I add a destination that isn't listed? +

Absolutely! Head over to our Destinations page and click "Add Destination". Fill in the details and it'll be added to our community list instantly.

Are the guides verified and safe? +

We take safety seriously. All guides go through a profile review process, and tourist reviews are verified. We continuously monitor ratings to maintain quality.
