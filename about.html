Our Story

About LocalGuide

We believe travel is better when it's personal. We built the platform that makes that possible.

Our Mission

Human Connection at the Heart of Every Journey

LocalGuide was born from a simple frustration: most travel platforms treat destinations like products and tourists like transactions. We wanted something different.

We built a platform where knowledgeable locals and curious travelers could genuinely connect — sharing stories, culture, and experiences that leave a lasting impact on both sides.

  • 🌍
    50+ destinations covered across 6 continents, with more added by our community every week.
  • 🤝
    200+ verified local guides who are passionate about sharing their home with the world.
  • 💛
    3,000+ happy tourists who experienced travel the way it was meant to be.
